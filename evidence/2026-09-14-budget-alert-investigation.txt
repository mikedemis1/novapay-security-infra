5. Why the forecast says 7.09
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AWS extrapolates from month-to-date. Two thirds of this month's 3.67 was spent
in the first six days, on resources that no longer exist. Extrapolating a
front-loaded month over the remaining days produces a number that has nothing
to do with the current run rate.

At 0.008 USD a day, the sixteen days left in September add about 0.13, so the
month ends near 3.80, not 7.09. October starts from zero and, at this run rate,
finishes around 0.25.


6. Conclusion
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No action. Nothing is running that should not be. The alert did its job: it
fired, the account was checked against real numbers, and the answer is that the
spend is historical.

One thing to expect rather than be surprised by: a FORECASTED threshold on an
account this cheap is sensitive to exactly this kind of front-loading, so it can
fire again after any month with a burst of activity early on. The ACTUAL
threshold at 1 USD is the one that means money has genuinely been spent. During
the AWS trial window of 10 to 16 October, expect both to fire and read the
ACTUAL one.
